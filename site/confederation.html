Confederation

Hold bitcoin together. Earn on it.

A confederation is a group of custodians who jointly control one Bitcoin address with FROST threshold signatures. Depositors receive sBTC 1:1 and can always burn it to get their BTC back. The creator earns 2.5% APY on all capital staked in the confederation, plus 7% APY on their own stake.

How a launch goes

  1. Request — you list the custodians and send a signed request from this page.
  2. Invitation — the operator publishes it on Ethereum; each custodian checks its code against the one the operator posts in Telegram.
  3. Installation — each custodian runs one script; it builds the software and waits for the invitation.
  4. Key ceremony — three distributed key generations, 2–3 days, no human steps.
  5. Custody — deposits, sBTC, staking; withdrawals signed by the custodian threshold.

What a custodian needs

  • A computer with an NVIDIA GPU of 6 GB or more (Linux, or Windows with WSL2), or a Mac on Apple Silicon.
  • 100 GB of free disk space and a stable internet connection, kept on during the launch.
  • A personal wallet (MetaMask or a hardware wallet) and an Ethereum RPC URL.
  • 0.1 ETH on the work address the script creates.
